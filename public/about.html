Why this exists

About this page

When Jackie was rescued, thousands of people who love her were left scrambling between comment threads, screenshots, and rumors. For Jackie gathers only what official sources have actually said, in one calm place, so nobody has to doomscroll for the truth.

The rules we follow

How facts get on this page

  • Official sources only

    A claim appears here only if it came from Friends of Big Bear Valley, the Ojai Raptor Center, an involved agency, or reporting that directly quotes them. Every claim links to where it was said.

  • Uncertainty is stated, not filled

    Where nothing official exists, this page says "unknown" rather than guessing. The unknowns list on the home page is a feature, not a gap.

  • Corrections beat pride

    If we get something wrong, or an official source supersedes us, we fix it fast and log the change. When this page and an official source disagree, trust the official source.
